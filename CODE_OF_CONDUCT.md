# Code of Conduct

Dockside follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want Dockside to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Help newcomers — everyone was new once.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others’ private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that don’t follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@shreyansa98](https://github.com/shreyansa98) privately through GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
